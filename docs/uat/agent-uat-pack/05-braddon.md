# Ruth and Mo · Braddon

**A decision while the engineer is still to report**

You're downsizing to a level-entry unit. You can put down $280,000, but you saw movement along one wall and the engineer has yet to issue a finding. Ruth likes the layout. Mo wants to know what you can decide today and what must wait.

### Make the decision as Ruth and Mo

1. **Before the report arrives.** Test the $640,000 offer, $400,000 cash before settlement and $10,000 total first-year works including urgent works. Leave **Has structural safety been cleared?** at **Unknown**. Record settlement cash, the property check, the offer-limit message and what you would tell the agent today.
2. **A cheaper offer.** With the structural answer still **Unknown**, test $635,000 for the offer, $411,000 cash before settlement and $7,000 total first-year works including urgent works. What can these figures settle for you? What evidence are you still waiting on?
3. **Two hypothetical calls from the engineer.** Return all three money fields to their original $640,000 / $400,000 / $10,000. Under **Adjust property checks**, change **Has structural safety been cleared?** to **Yes** for the first call and test. Then change it to **No** for the second call and test again. Record both decisions and their settlement cash. These are alternative stories. Explain what you would do in each, whom you would contact, and which answer you would leave on your real decision sheet while no report exists.
